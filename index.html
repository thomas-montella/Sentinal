Sole-Source Apocalypse

AMPAC in Cedar City, UT is the only US producer of ammonium perchlorate — the oxidizer in every solid-fuel US missile. One fire stops all Patriot, AMRAAM, Minuteman III, and Trident production simultaneously.

920 Pounds of Leverage

Each F-35 contains 920 lbs of rare earth materials. Each Virginia-class submarine requires 9,200 lbs. China controls 90%+ of global rare earth processing — and export controls are already active.

The ASML Problem

One Dutch company — ASML — manufactures 100% of the world's EUV lithography machines needed to produce any chip smaller than 7nm. There is no substitute. There is no second source. There will not be one for a decade.

Artillery Shell Gap

The US produces ~30,000 155mm shells per month. Estimated wartime need: 90,000+/month. Ukraine proved the math — and the gap has not closed. NATO is producing at 8-12% of wartime requirement.
