WEAPONS PLATFORMS

22 US defense platforms assessed for supply chain vulnerability. Composite risk scores calculated across materials supply, geopolitical exposure, industrial base capacity, and logistics risk.

CRITICAL ≥ 9.0 HIGH ≥ 7.5 MEDIUM ≥ 6.0 LOW < 6.0 Scoring: Materials ×0.30 + Industrial ×0.25 + Geopolitical ×0.25 + Logistics ×0.20
Key Finding (Govini 2025): More than 80,000 parts across 1,900 weapon systems incorporate antimony, gallium, germanium, tungsten, or tellurium — meaning 78% of all DoD weapon systems are potentially affected by China's export bans. Over 91% of Navy weapon systems rely on these materials.
F-35 Lightning II
USAF / USN / USMC · Lockheed Martin
9.6
⚗️ 917–920 lbs rare earth content per aircraft
Critical materials: Neodymium/Dysprosium (flight control actuators), Terbium (magnets), Gallium (AESA radar), Germanium (IR sensors), Samarium-Cobalt (turbomachine magnets)
🔴 ACTIVE: F-35 deliveries halted 2022 — Chinese SmCo magnet found in Honeywell turbomachine. REE controls 2025 tightening.
B-21 Raider
USAF Stealth Bomber · Northrop Grumman
9.0
⚗️ ~800–1,000 lbs REE content (est.)
Critical materials: Titanium (airframe), Neodymium/Dysprosium (actuators), Gallium (AESA radar), Advanced chips ≤5nm (mission computing), Graphite (stealth coatings)
🟠 PARTIAL: REE export controls apply; chip supply strained; titanium 100% import-dependent.
F-22 Raptor
USAF Air Superiority · Lockheed Martin
9.1
⚗️ ~800 lbs REE content (est.)
Critical materials: Titanium (60% of airframe by weight), Neodymium/Dysprosium (flight controls), Gallium (radar), Rhenium (F119 turbine blades), Beryllium (avionics)
🟠 PARTIAL: Production ended 2011; sustainment REE/titanium components at risk.
F-16 Fighting Falcon
USAF / FMS Multirole · Lockheed Martin
9.1
⚗️ ~300 lbs REE content (est.)
Critical materials: Antimony (flame-resistant systems — 81% China-linked), Gallium (radar), Titanium (structural), Germanium (sensors)
🔴 ACTIVE: Antimony BAN Dec 2024 directly affects F-16. Chinese materials found in F-16 supply chain (2014).
MQ-9 Reaper / MQ-1C
USAF / USA Armed UAV · General Atomics
7.9
⚗️ ~50–100 lbs REE + battery materials
Critical materials: Lithium (battery packs), Gallium (comms/EW), REE magnets (actuators), Advanced chips (mission processing), Cobalt (batteries)
🟠 ACTIVE: Li-ion battery supply China-processed; GaN electronics strained; CATL blacklisted Jan 2025.
Virginia-Class SSN
USN Nuclear Submarine · GD Electric Boat / HII
9.8
⚗️ 9,200 lbs (~4.2 metric tons) rare earth content
Critical materials: Neodymium/Dysprosium (electric drive motors — 2–4 kg each), Terbium (Terfenol-D sonar), Titanium (hull), Cobalt (superalloys), Gallium (electro-optic sensors)
🔴 ACTIVE: REE export controls constrain motor magnet supply. Only 2 US shipyards; running at ~1.3 boats/yr vs 2/yr target.
Columbia-Class SSBN
USN Strategic Deterrent · GD Electric Boat
9.8
⚗️ 9,000+ lbs REE content (est.)
Critical materials: Neodymium/Dysprosium (propulsion), Terbium (sonar), Titanium (hull), HEU (nuclear fuel), Specialty electronics (launch systems)
🔴 CRITICAL: Strategic nuclear deterrent — delivery delays directly threaten deterrence posture. Same REE constraints as Virginia-class.
Arleigh Burke DDG
USN Surface Combatant · GD BIW / HII Ingalls
9.5
⚗️ 5,200 lbs (~2.3 metric tons) rare earth content

🔴 ACTIVE: Govini explicitly names Arleigh Burke among top-4 most impacted systems by Dec-2024 mineral bans.

8.5

🟠 PARTIAL: EMALS catapult REE motors constrained. USS Kennedy (CVN-79) sea trials 2025.
M1A2 Abrams SEPv3

9.1

🔴 ACTIVE: Tungsten BAN Jan 2025 hits 120mm ammunition. Nitroguanidine 100% imported — zero US production.
