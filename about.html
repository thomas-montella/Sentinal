ABOUT SENTINEL

Methodology, scoring model, data sources, and project background. SENTINEL is an open-source defense supply chain intelligence project using publicly available government, academic, and financial data.

What Is SENTINEL?

SENTINEL is a defense supply chain intelligence platform that aggregates and analyzes publicly available data on the critical material dependencies, geopolitical chokepoints, and industrial base vulnerabilities that determine whether the United States can build, maintain, and deploy its most advanced weapons systems.

The project spans 15 data modules covering 27 critical materials, 22 weapon platforms, 13 supplier nations, 12 key companies, 5 wargame scenarios, and 11 technology substitution pathways. All data is sourced from publicly available government reports, financial filings, academic research, and news sources. Nothing in this project is classified.

Composite Risk Scoring Model

Each material and weapons system is scored on six dimensions, each rated 1–10 (10 = highest risk). Dimension weights are adjustable for different threat scenarios.

Default Balanced Weighting
S1 — Materials & Components Supply25%
S2 — Geopolitical & Alliance Risk20%
S3 — Industrial Base Capacity20%
S4 — Logistics & Transport15%
S5 — Economic & Financial Stability10%
S6 — Regulatory & Policy Environment10%

Weapons platform scoring uses a different weighting: Materials ×0.30 + Industrial Base ×0.25 + Geopolitical ×0.25 + Logistics ×0.20, reflecting the more direct material dependency of hardware systems.

The full scoring engine with adjustable scenario weights is available as an Excel workbook download (see below). Five pre-built scenarios — China conflict, industrial surge, logistics crisis, economic warfare, and balanced peacetime — adjust weights to model different threat environments.

Risk Band Definitions

CRITICAL — Score ≥ 9.0
Immediate action required. Active disruptions likely or in progress. Wartime production or strategic operations at direct risk. No viable near-term alternative.
HIGH — Score 7.5–8.9
Significant vulnerability. Disruption probable under adversarial conditions. Mitigation programs needed within 1–3 years to avoid production impact.
MEDIUM — Score 6.0–7.4
Moderate vulnerability. Supply disruption possible but manageable. Monitor and develop contingency plans. Substitution or diversification possible within 3–5 years.
LOW — Score < 6.0
Manageable risk. Multiple supply sources or strong domestic capability. Standard procurement practices adequate. Monitor for deterioration.

Project Scope & Limitations

SENTINEL uses publicly available data and cannot incorporate classified intelligence assessments, specific classified stockpile levels, or non-public DoD acquisition data. Risk scores represent analytical estimates based on open-source information and should be treated as directional assessments, not definitive security evaluations.

All supply disruption scenarios are analytical constructs for understanding vulnerability — not predictions of specific events. Wargame scenarios are based on publicly available research from CSIS, RAND, SAIS, and other open-source think tanks.

Data is current as of May 2026. The supply chain landscape changes rapidly — China's export control regime has escalated every quarter since December 2024. Figures should be verified against current sources for operational decision-making.
