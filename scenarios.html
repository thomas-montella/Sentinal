WARGAME SCENARIOS

Five conflict scenarios modeled for supply chain cascade failure. Each scenario maps trigger events to Day 1, Week 1, Month 1, and Year 1 impacts — identifying which weapon systems go offline first and at what cost.

Based on: CSIS 'Lights Out?' Taiwan Blockade Wargame (26 iterations, Jul 2025) · SAIS Review Dec-2025 · FPRI Oct-2025 · War on the Rocks Aug-2025
Methodology: Each scenario is based on publicly available wargame analysis, defense reports, and supply chain data. Timelines represent synthesized estimates from multiple sources. These are analytical tools for understanding vulnerability — not predictions of specific events.
🇨🇳
China Taiwan Strait Blockade
PLA Navy blockades Taiwan Strait — TSMC offline, all REE/chip exports halted simultaneously
CRITICAL
▼
Day 1
• TSMC Taiwan fabs inaccessible
• REE magnet exports to US cease
• Ga/Ge/Sb bans enforced at 100%
• Global chip spot prices +200–400%
Week 1
• F-35 actuator magnet stockpile drawdown begins
• GaN radar T/R module production halts
• South Korea memory chip exports rerouted
• Japan/Korea ally sourcing activated
Month 1
• DoD heavy REE stockpile (Dy/Tb) depleting
• Advanced chip shortage hits missile guidance computers
• AMRAAM/Javelin/Patriot production -30–50%
• Emergency AUKUS material sharing activated
Year 1
• F-35 production falls toward zero without new REE magnets
• Advanced UAV production severely constrained
• $2.7T estimated global GDP loss from chip disruption
• Allied industrial base partially mobilized
First Systems Offline
AN/TPY-2 radar (GaN) · F-35 APG-81 AESA · Javelin (Ge seeker) · New Virginia-class SSNs
Degraded by 6 Months
F-35 production · AMRAAM (AP+GaN+Ge) · Columbia-class SSBN · All advanced guided munitions
Estimated Economic Impact
$2.7T GDP (Year 1) + $500B defense production losses
Recovery Timeline
Chips: 5–8 years · REE magnets: 3–5 years · Full recovery: 8–10 years
Mitigation Actions
Activate NDS stockpile immediately · Emergency AUKUS/Quad material sharing · DPA Title III emergency production orders · Accelerate MP Materials/Lynas USA · Begin chip rationing for DoD priority programs

High-Intensity Conventional War — Munitions Surge
Large-scale conventional conflict (e.g., NATO Article 5) — all munitions production must surge simultaneously to wartime rates
CRITICAL
▼
Day 1
• AP (AMPAC) immediately becomes binding constraint on ALL PGMs
• 155mm shell demand 8–10x current capacity
• GMLRS rockets depleted within days at wartime rate
• HMX/RDX demand spikes 10x Holston capacity
Week 1
• AP shortage forces triage: AMRAAM vs Patriot vs Minuteman vs HIMARS
• Artillery shell shortage mirrors Ukraine experience at larger scale
• DPA invoked; civilian factories recruited for shell casings
• TNT shortage emerges (10–15x below wartime need)
Month 1
• US fires through precision munition stockpile
• Holston AAP running 24/7 but still 50% of wartime need
• DoD requests allied manufacturing (Poland, Japan coproduction)
• Nitrocellulose wood-pulp pivot urgent
Year 1
• PGM production at 30–40% of wartime consumption rate
• Artillery shell production at 8–12% of wartime need
• US reaches strategic munitions shortage threshold
• Adversary gains conventional fire superiority
First Systems Offline
ALL solid-fuel PGMs (AP constraint) · 155mm artillery (production gap) · GMLRS rockets (AP + demand surge)
Degraded by 6 Months
AMRAAM · Patriot PAC-3 · Javelin · HIMARS GMLRS · All artillery munitions
Annual Production Gap
$300–500B annual munitions production gap vs wartime need
Recovery Timeline
Munitions surge: 2–3 years · AP expansion: 18 months · Full: 3–5 years
Priority Actions (Must Start NOW)
Immediately begin AMPAC second-facility construction · Pre-negotiate surge contracts with all munitions primes · Fund Warstopper program 10x per DBB recommendation · Build 180-day AP/HMX/RDX strategic reserve · Qualify wood-pulp NC at scale

Economic Warfare — China Market Manipulation
China floods cobalt/lithium/graphite/magnesium markets for 3–5 years, bankrupting all Western producers (Jervois 2025 precedent replicated at scale)
HIGH (Slow-Burn)
▼
Year 1
• Commodity prices collapse across Co/Li/graphite/Mg
• Western mining/processing projects shelved (uneconomical)
• PE-backed defense suppliers exit market
• China market share quietly grows 70% → 90%+
Year 2
• Albemarle cuts Li capex further
• Syrah Technologies graphite unable to raise capital
• Sub-tier defense suppliers exit market quietly
• DoD supply chain audits reveal increasing Chinese penetration
Year 3
• DoD discovers 2–3 additional sole-source situations created by manipulation
• Battery supply for military UAVs now 90%+ China-dependent
• DPA investments insufficient to offset market forces
• Military Li-ion battery production falls
Year 5+
• China achieves near-monopoly on Li/Co/graphite processing
• Any future conflict activates immediate supply cutoff capability
• US defense battery supply entirely China-dependent
• No market-based solution exists — only government intervention
Systems Eventually at Risk
MQ-9 Reaper (batteries) · Soldier electronics (IVAS, radios) · Forward base power systems · All UAV categories
Proof of Concept (Realized)
Jervois Global — the only US cobalt mine — went bankrupt in 2025 after Chinese price manipulation. This scenario already happened once.
Long-Term Remediation Cost
$200–400B to rebuild domestic mining/processing base
Recovery Timeline
10–15 years to rebuild competitive domestic mining/processing base. Longest recovery of any scenario.
⚠️ This Scenario Is Already In Progress
China has deployed this exact strategy against cobalt. Jervois Global is bankrupt. The same pattern is now running against lithium, graphite, and magnesium. DoD must act with price floors, strategic purchases, and DPA investments before more companies fail — not after.
