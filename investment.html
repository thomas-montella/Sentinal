MODULE 04

INVESTMENT INTELLIGENCE

Financial health profiles of 12 key supplier companies — from DoD strategic partners to active threat actors. Covers revenue, DoD contracts, bankruptcy risk, and strategic significance to US defense supply chains.

NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE — For supply chain risk research purposes only. Sources: SEC 8-K/10-K filings 2025–2026, public financial disclosures.
Key Pattern to Watch: China's strategy is to flood markets with below-cost pricing → bankrupt Western competitors → achieve monopoly → activate supply cutoff. Jervois Global (the only US cobalt mine) went bankrupt in 2025 — proof the strategy works. The same pattern is actively targeting lithium (Albemarle under pressure) and graphite (Syrah facing challenges).
🇺🇸 US STRATEGIC PARTNERS & DoD-BACKED COMPANIES
MP Materials
NYSE: MP — DoD is largest shareholder
STRONG
Revenue: $291M (2025) +49% YoY · Q1-2026: $90.6M (+49% YoY)
Only integrated US rare earth producer. Mountain Pass mine (CA) + Independence magnet plant (TX). Transformational 2025: DoD $400M equity stake, $150M loan, 10-year 100% magnet offtake, $110/kg NdPr price floor. Apple offtake signed. Ceased all China sales Jul 2025.
⚡ Strategic significance: The single most important domestic defense supply chain investment of the decade. MP's success or failure determines whether the US can build F-35s and Virginia-class submarines without Chinese rare earths. DoD equity stake = implicit guarantee. Watch: Northlake TX magnet campus breaking ground 2026 — 10,000 MT/yr target by 2028.
Lynas Rare Earths
ASX: LYC — Australian listed, AUKUS partner
GOOD
Revenue: ~A$900M (FY2025 est.) — profitable, government-backed
Largest non-Chinese REE producer globally. Lynas USA (Seadrift, TX) provides US-based heavy REE separation — directly addresses the Dy/Tb processing bottleneck that enables Chinese leverage. DoD has invested in Lynas USA facility. AUKUS partner.
⚡ Strategic significance: Critical complement to MP Materials — processes heavy REEs (Dy, Tb) that MP is still developing capability for. AUSFTA enables streamlined DoD procurement. Building additional processing capacity 2025–2027. Key risk: ASX-listed means less DoD direct control.
Micron Technology
NASDAQ: MU — US-listed
GOOD
Revenue: ~$25B (FY2025) — profitable; AI-driven DRAM surge
Only major US memory chip manufacturer. New fabs in Syracuse NY and Boise ID under CHIPS Act. Indian facility (Gujarat) began partial ops early 2025. HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) for AI accelerators = massive growth driver. Defense-grade memory specs require certified domestic supplier — Micron is the only option.
⚡ Strategic significance: $6.1B CHIPS Act award. If Micron exits or weakens, US has no domestic memory chip fallback. Watch: HBM competitiveness vs Samsung/SK Hynix for AI accelerators — this determines whether CHIPS Act succeeds.
ATI Inc. (Allegheny Technologies)
NYSE: ATI — US-listed
GOOD
Revenue: ~$4.2B (2024) — profitable, defense backlog strong
Key US specialty titanium alloy and nickel superalloy processor. Supplies Ti-6Al-4V for F-35, F-22, next-gen aircraft. Benefits from titanium reshoring push as Russia/Kazakhstan sources disrupted. ITAR-controlled qualified source for defense titanium alloys.
⚡ Strategic significance: One of only 2–3 US specialty titanium processors. Adding ATI to ITAR-controlled approved source list takes 2+ years — ATI is already on it. Defense revenue share growing as reshoring accelerates. Quietly critical to US airpower.
Materion Corporation
NYSE: MTRN — US-listed
GOOD
Revenue: ~$1.3B (2024) — profitable, niche dominance
Dominant global beryllium supplier (Elmore, OH). Beryllium's extreme toxicity naturally limits competition — Materion IS the US beryllium supply chain. Supplies DoD nuclear components, sonar, X-ray systems. NDS beryllium reserves are Materion-supplied.
⚡ Strategic significance: Unique case — US dominance in beryllium is a strategic ADVANTAGE. Risk is NOT Chinese control — it's that Materion is a single company. Any fire, labor action, or contamination incident at the Ohio facility has no domestic fallback. DoD should fund a strategic reserve of finished beryllium products.
IperionX
ASX: IPX — Australian, AUKUS-aligned
DEVELOPING
Revenue: ~$30–50M (early-stage, scaling)
Reshoring US titanium sponge supply chain. Awarded $99M DoD SBIR Phase III contract. First US Army task order delivered 2025. Novel low-cost, low-emissions titanium process vs. legacy Kroll process. Addresses 100% US import dependency on titanium sponge.
⚡ Strategic significance: If IperionX succeeds at scale, it solves one of the most acute US defense supply chain vulnerabilities — titanium sponge 100% import dependency. Risk: early-stage company must execute complex scale-up on defense timeline. Watch production milestones closely.
⚠️ DISTRESSED / HIGH-RISK COMPANIES
Wolfspeed
NYSE: WOLF — post-Chapter 11 bankruptcy 2025
POST-BANKRUPTCY
Revenue: $807M (FY2024) · Net loss: -$864M (FY2024) · Restructuring ongoing
US-based silicon carbide (SiC) and GaN semiconductor manufacturer — critical for military radar, EW, and power electronics. Filed Chapter 11 in 2025 after EV market slowdown and capital costs overwhelmed balance sheet. Emerged with restructured debt. $750M CHIPS Act grant + $900M+ 48D tax credits provide lifeline.
⚠️ Critical warning: A US-based SiC/GaN supplier nearly went bankrupt — this is exactly the financial vulnerability the model predicts. Post-restructuring: AI/defense revenue surging, debt cut 25% Q3-FY26. But negative operating cash flow persists. If Wolfspeed fails again, US loses its only domestic SiC supplier for military radar and EW systems. DoD must maintain demand signal.
Albemarle Corporation
NYSE: ALB — US-listed
MODERATE
Revenue: ~$4.3B (2024) — down from $9.6B peak 2023; restructuring underway
Largest US-linked lithium processor. Chile and Australia operations. Lithium price collapsed 70%+ from 2022 peak — devastated financials. Capex slashed, workforce reduced. DoE and DoD engagement ongoing but no equity stake yet.
⚠️ Warning — watch closely: China flooded lithium market → prices collapsed → Albemarle severely weakened. If ALB exits lithium processing, US defense battery supply has no domestic fallback. This is the Jervois pattern playing out in slow motion. DoD should replicate the MP Materials model — price floor + equity stake — before Albemarle's situation deteriorates further.
🔴 CAUTIONARY TALES — FAILURE CASES
Jervois Global
ASX: JRV — BANKRUPT 2025
BANKRUPT
Revenue: $0 — mine closed 2023, company bankrupt 2025
Was the only US cobalt mine (Idaho Cobalt Operations). Closed 2023 after Chinese cobalt price manipulation drove prices below operating costs. Filed bankruptcy 2025. US now has zero domestic cobalt production.
🔴 This is the worst-case scenario realized. China deliberately flooded cobalt market → drove down prices → made Idaho mine uneconomical → forced closure → bankruptcy. The only US cobalt mine is gone. DoD now has ZERO domestic cobalt production for jet engine superalloys and UAV batteries. This exact pattern is now being repeated against lithium, graphite, and magnesium. Jervois is the proof of concept for China's economic warfare strategy.
🇨🇳 THREAT ACTOR PROFILES — Chinese State-Linked Entities
CMOC Group
HK: 3993 — Chinese state-linked, DRC mining
THREAT ACTOR
Revenue: ~$20B+ (2024) — state-backed, growing
Chinese state-linked firm controlling 24% of global cobalt mining in the DRC. DoD blacklisted CATL (battery integrator) Jan 2025; CMOC feeds into CATL supply chains. Tibet Huayu (CMOC-linked) operates in Tajikistan — entrenching supply chain control globally.
🔴 Threat profile: CMOC represents China's "quiet" supply chain control — not via direct export bans but via upstream mine ownership globally. Govini: 88% of supply chains for key materials have at least one Chinese vendor within 6 tiers. CMOC is embedded in global cobalt supply chains in ways US defense contractors cannot easily audit or exclude.
CATL (Contemporary Amperex)
SZ: 300750 — DoD BLACKLISTED Jan 2025
BLACKLISTED
Revenue: ~$45B+ (2024) — world's largest battery manufacturer
World's largest battery manufacturer, controlling ~70% of global EV battery production. DoD blacklisted January 2025 as an agent of the Chinese military. Was previously embedded in US military supply chains through sub-tier suppliers. Dominates LFP battery chemistry.
🔴 Threat profile: CATL's blacklisting exposed how deeply Chinese battery technology was embedded in US defense systems — often without prime contractors' knowledge. The blacklisting forces supply chain redesign but creates near-term battery shortages for US military UAVs and soldier electronics. No equivalent-scale US or allied battery manufacturer exists.
